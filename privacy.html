Privacy Policy for PiPrompt Studio

Last updated: November 27, 2026

PiPrompt Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") is a prompt builder and curated template hub designed for Pi creators and builders. We respect your privacy and keep data collection minimal.

1. Information We Collect

PiPrompt Studio does not require personal registration to browse prompt templates. If you connect through the Pi Browser or Pi App Platform, basic public Pi profile or session details may only be used when needed for app functionality or testing on Pi Testnet.

2. Local Storage & Browser Data

Your preferences, drafts, or selected prompts may be stored locally in your browser (such as localStorage) to improve your experience. We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services & Links

The app runs on GitHub Pages and may reference Pi Network services or external links. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

4. Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as PiPrompt Studio evolves. For questions or support, please reach out through our official project repository or community channels.
